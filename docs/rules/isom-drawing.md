# ISOM drawing specifications

Digest of the graphic definitions in ISOM 2017-2 rev. 6 (see [`docs/sources`](../sources/README.md)), for recognising symbols by colour, line width, dash pattern and size when importing a map from PDF or an image. Movement and control-site rules are in [`isom-symbols.md`](isom-symbols.md). Sources are cited as `§` for sections and `sym.` for symbol definitions; values read from figure labels rather than text are cited as `fig.`, and §3.8 is the magnified "precise definition" sheet. Lines marked **Interpretation** are our own reading, not ISOM text.

## Scale and units

- All dimensions are in mm on the printed map at the base scale 1:15 000 (§2.9, §3 note).
- An enlarged map enlarges all lines, symbols and screens proportionally: 150% at 1:10 000 (§2.9.1). Graphical minimum dimensions scale the same way (§2.11.3).
- **Interpretation:** for a map printed at 1:S, multiply every value below by 15 000 / S. Map scale must be known (or estimated from the magnetic north line spacing, 601) before matching widths.
- Figures in §3.1–3.6 are drawn at 1:7 500, i.e. twice size; §3.8 figures are 10× (§3, §3.8). Measuring figures is not a source; only labelled values are used here.
- Label suffixes (§3 legend): (OM) outside measure, (IM) inside measure, (CC) centre to centre, ø diameter. A plain number next to a line is its width.
- Dashed lines: gaps are always as specified; dashes may be shortened to no less than 0.8 × the given length, and start and end dashes should be equal (§2.11.3 "Rendering of dashed lines"). Dotted lines: gap ≥ 0.8 × the given gap. Styled lines (tags, dots on a line): spacing ≥ 0.8 × given, end length half the spacing (§2.11.3).
- **Interpretation:** a recogniser should accept dash lengths from 0.8× to about 1× nominal, and gaps close to nominal.
- Unspecified minimum widths of areas: 100% green 0.25, 100% yellow 0.3, colour screens 0.4 (§2.11.3).

## Colours

Colour definitions (CMYK, spot colours) are in a separate document, _IOF Map Specifications – Printing and Colour Definitions_ (§2.12), which we don't have. ISOM itself gives only colour names and screen percentages; the one numeric value is dark green for 416, CMYK 100/0/80/30 (Errata 23.01.2022, 416). Group colours per §3: landforms brown, rock black + grey, water blue, vegetation green + yellow, man-made black, technical black + blue, course planning purple.
